About Me

I have been learning music for 20 years and teaching for 7 years. I perform regularly at various shows and have sung with famous playback singers including Vijay Prakash, Ramya Behara (voice behind "Dheevara"), KS Chitra, and many other well-known artists.

I teach Carnatic music, light music, and film songs through personalized one-on-one lessons designed for your goals and level. Classes are mainly online, in-person options available for local students(Hyderabad).

Whether you're a beginner discovering your voice or an advanced learner refining your craft, I'm committed to helping you achieve your musical aspirations through structured, individual attention.
